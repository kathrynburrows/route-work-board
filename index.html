Work rules

One work item per

Groups fill in drop order: each route's stops in RoadNet stop order, then the next route. A pick closes at the target (up to 5% over), and can carry over into the next route when sheets are dropped together. Consolidation pairs stay together, even out of stop sequence. A single stop heavier than that gets its own pick.

Only use rows where

Leave empty to use every row. Conditions apply to new drops.

Auto priority

Applies only when a route first comes in. You can always flip Y/N by hand.

Home patient consolidation

Home patient = 7-digit ship-to starting with 2. Two orders per pallet at most. Orders flagged together always stay in the same pick.
